1. The inputs

The models use 12 factors that affect whether chemicals wash away:

GroupInputs
Soilsoil moisture, soil nitrogen, clay fraction
Landslope, buffer strip width, distance to water
Weatherforecast rain for the next 24 h and 72 h
Farm activitydays since fertilizer and pesticide, and the amounts used

2. The prediction models

Two Gradient Boosting regression models, one for nitrogen and one for pesticide, each output a risk score from 0 to 1. Gradient Boosting builds many small decision trees, each correcting the mistakes of the last, which suits mixed, non-linear tabular data like this.

3. The warning engine

Risk scoreLevel
below 0.25LOW
0.25 to 0.50MEDIUM
0.50 and aboveHIGH

Each level maps to actions such as postponing fertilizer, avoiding spraying for 48 hours, or widening a buffer strip.

4. The fertilizer plan

Soil supply is estimated from soil nitrogen. The crop's nitrogen demand minus that supply gives the deficit, which is split into three doses. If heavy rain is forecast or risk is high, the dose is delayed or cut by 30%.

5. The website

The dashboard sends your slider values to a Flask API. The server runs the trained models and returns the result as JSON, and the page updates instantly. The risk-vs-rainfall chart asks the same models for 16 rainfall values.

About this project

AI-Assisted Early Warning System for Precision Fertilizer Management and Pesticide Runoff Mitigation

Progress so far

✅ Prediction models

Two Gradient Boosting models for nitrogen and pesticide runoff risk.

✅ Fertilizer advice

Crop demand versus soil supply, with split and delayed doses.

✅ Early warning engine

Low, Medium, High levels with actions.

✅ Website and alerts

Flask site, live dashboard, SMS and WhatsApp alerts via Twilio.

Results

≈0.82R² nitrogen model
≈0.82R² pesticide model
≈0.02mean absolute error

Limitation

The data is synthetic, generated from agronomic and hydrological relationships. The accuracy shows the pipeline works, not real-world performance. Alerts run as a dry run unless Twilio credentials are set.

Common questions

Why synthetic data?

Real labelled runoff data is scarce and expensive, so the pipeline was built first and will be validated with real data next.

Why Gradient Boosting?

It handles non-linear relationships and mixed features well on tabular data and needs less data than deep learning.

Why call it AI-assisted and not just rules?

The risk prediction is learned by the model. Rules only translate predicted risk into actions.

How would a farmer use it?

Through the dashboard, or a WhatsApp alert before rain is forecast.

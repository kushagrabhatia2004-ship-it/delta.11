Open the dashboard  How it works

The problem

When heavy rain follows fertilizer or pesticide application, the chemicals wash into rivers and ponds. This wastes money, pollutes water and harms ecosystems, and farmers rarely get a warning in time to act.

What the system does

1Predicts risk

Two machine learning models estimate nitrogen and pesticide runoff risk from 12 field conditions.

2Gives advice

Turns Low, Medium or High risk into plain actions, like "do not spray for 48 hours".

3Plans fertilizer

Compares what the crop needs with what the soil already has, then applies, reduces or delays.

4Sends alerts

Warnings can go to the farmer's phone by SMS or WhatsApp.
